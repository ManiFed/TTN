Your telescope is installed. Here is what to do now.

1. Make sure your telescope is on your home Wi-Fi.
In the telescope's own app this is called Station Mode or “join network”. Out of the box most smart telescopes make their own Wi-Fi network, and while they are doing that this computer cannot see them. The telescope's app says “connected” either way, so it will not warn you.

2. Talk to it.
A page should have opened at http://localhost:5173/chat — if it did not, open that address in your browser. Type or say:

“connect my telescope”

It will find the telescope, link it to your account (making one for you if you do not have one), and tell you when it is online. That is the whole setup.

Already use Claude Desktop, Cursor or Windsurf?
Your telescope has been added to it as well. Quit and reopen it — it only notices new tools on restart — then say the same thing there. Either way works; use whichever you prefer.

The node software is already running in the background and will keep running after you close everything. It observes on its own every clear night once your telescope is connected — you do not need to be awake for it.

Nothing happening? The page at http://localhost:5173/chat can diagnose itself — ask it “is anything wrong?”. Or email info@boundlessskies.org.
