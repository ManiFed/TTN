Installed. Two things left.

1. Put your telescope on your home Wi-Fi.
In the telescope's own app this is called Station Mode or “join network”. Out of the box most smart telescopes make their own Wi-Fi network, and while they are doing that this computer cannot see them. The telescope's app says “connected” either way, so it will not warn you.

2. Open Claude Desktop and say:

“set up my telescope”

It will take you through the rest — finding the telescope, making you an account if you need one, and telling you when it is online. You do not need to know what comes next; it will tell you at each point.

If Claude Desktop was already open, quit and reopen it. It only notices new tools when it starts, so your telescope will not appear until you do.

Do not have Claude Desktop? It is free to install from https://claude.ai/download. Install it, open it, and say the same thing — your telescope is already set up inside it and will be waiting.

The node software is already running in the background and keeps running after you close everything. Once your telescope is connected it observes on its own every clear night — you do not need to be awake for it.
