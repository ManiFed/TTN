Welcome to The Telescope Net

This installer sets up the Telescope Net desktop app and its always-on local node service.

The app is your control surface. The node service runs silently in the background, connecting your telescope to the TTN network and contributing science-quality photometry to the global variable star record.

Run it by asking

If you use an AI assistant such as Claude Desktop, this installer connects your telescope to it — nothing for you to configure. Afterwards you can say “connect my telescope”, “what’s the plan tonight?” or “show me what it imaged”, and stop it any time by saying so. You will need to quit and reopen the assistant once, so it notices the new tools. The desktop app works exactly as before either way.

Works with your telescope

TTN supports a wide range of equipment, including:

  • Smart telescopes — ZWO Seestar S50 & S30, Vaonis Vespera / Vespera II / Vespera Pro / Stellina / Hyperia, DwarfLab Dwarf II / Dwarf 3 / Dwarf S, Unistellar eVscope / eVscope 2 / eQuinox / eQuinox 2 / Odyssey, Celestron Origin
  • Traditional & GOTO setups — Celestron, Meade, Sky-Watcher, William Optics, Takahashi, and many more via ALPACA/ASCOM

After installation, the setup wizard will let you pick a star catalog for accurate plate solving — a smaller one (~200 MB) for most setups, or a larger one (~1.25 GB) for narrow-field imaging. You choose and download it yourself, and can switch later.

Requirements

  • macOS 11 or later
  • Your telescope connected to your local WiFi or via USB

Everything else — Python, the plate solver, all science libraries — is bundled. No separate downloads required.
