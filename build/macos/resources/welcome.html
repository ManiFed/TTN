Welcome to The Telescope Net

This installer sets up the Telescope Net node software — the always-on service that runs your telescope.

It observes on its own every clear night, connecting your telescope to the TTN network and contributing science-quality photometry to the global variable star record. You do not have to be awake for any of it.

You run it by asking. When this finishes, a page opens where you can talk to your telescope in plain language — “connect my telescope”, “what’s the plan tonight?”, “show me what it imaged”. If you already use an AI assistant such as Claude Desktop, Cursor or Windsurf, your telescope is set up in that too, so you can use whichever you prefer.

Works with your telescope

TTN supports a wide range of equipment, including:

  • Smart telescopes — ZWO Seestar S50 & S30, Vaonis Vespera / Vespera II / Vespera Pro / Stellina / Hyperia, DwarfLab Dwarf II / Dwarf 3 / Dwarf S, Unistellar eVscope / eVscope 2 / eQuinox / eQuinox 2 / Odyssey, Celestron Origin
  • Traditional & GOTO setups — Celestron, Meade, Sky-Watcher, William Optics, Takahashi, and many more via ALPACA/ASCOM

After you connect your telescope, your assistant will offer to install a star catalog for accurate plate solving — a smaller one (~200 MB) for most setups, or a larger one (~1.25 GB) for narrow-field imaging. Just tell it which one; it downloads and installs it for you, and you can switch later.

Requirements

  • macOS 11 or later
  • Your telescope joined to your home Wi-Fi in Station Mode — not making its own network. Its app will say “connected” either way, so check before you start.
  • An AI assistant that runs on this computer (such as Claude Desktop, Cursor, or Windsurf) — this is how you'll talk to your telescope

Everything else — Python, the plate solver, all science libraries — is bundled. No separate downloads required.
